蝶韵

Dieyun · Blue Agate Brooch

蓝玛瑙胸针

产品故事

主石：水滴形弧面蓝玛瑙，清透温润的湖蓝色调，肌理匀净光润，是阜新玛瑙中极具代表性的特色料种，也是这款吊坠的核心价值载体。

金属与造型：主石以简约包边镶嵌，顶部设计为舒展写意的蝶翼蝴蝶结造型，线条轻盈灵动，呼应 "蝶韵" 的命名 —— 取 "蝶翼轻扬、东方灵韵" 的传统蝶恋花意象，以现代极简手法转译，褪去传统纹样的冗繁，只留灵动气韵。

结构细节：顶部预留挂环结构，为标准吊坠款式；蛋面弧面最大化呈现玛瑙的通透质感与天然纹理，整体造型纤细雅致，适配日常佩戴，兼具东方意蕴与现代审美。

Main Stone: A teardrop cabochon blue agate in a clear, warm lake-blue hue with a smooth, lustrous texture. It is a highly representative signature variety of Fuxin agate and serves as the core value carrier of this pendant.

Metalwork & Design: The central stone is set in a minimalist bezel, topped with a graceful, freehand butterfly-bow motif with light, flowing lines. The design echoes the name Dieyun (Butterfly Charm), drawing from the classic Chinese "butterfly and flower" aesthetic of delicate grace and oriental allure. Reinterpreted through a modern minimalist approach, it strips away the ornateness of traditional patterns while retaining its vivid, ethereal spirit.

Structural Details: A bail is reserved at the top for a standard pendant design. The domed cabochon cut maximizes the agate's translucent texture and natural grain. With a slender, elegant silhouette ideal for everyday wear, it balances oriental artistic conception with contemporary aesthetics.

产地

辽宁阜新

解说

一方冰润蓝玛瑙，藏着山涧湖光的清透，色如晴空映水，晕开一层淡蓝烟岚，温润澄澈，肌理天然。

设计师取传统 "蝶恋花" 之意，以水墨写意之笔，化繁复蝶纹为简约蝶翼，轻落于石顶。线条舒展如蝶翼振风，形态轻灵似掠水而过，没有浓墨重彩的雕琢，只留一缕蝶影翩跹的意趣。

匠人循石之形细细打磨，将手绘稿上的轻盈笔意，凝作方寸间的温润实物。戴于颈间，便似携了一汪山涧清蓝、一羽林间蝶影，清雅自现，余韵悠然。

This icy, luminous blue agate holds the clarity of mountain lakes. Its hue evokes a clear sky reflected in water, diffused like pale blue mist across rice paper — warm, pure, with a naturally formed texture.

Drawing inspiration from the classic "butterfly and flower" motif in Chinese aesthetics, the designer applies the freehand spirit of ink wash painting, transforming elaborate butterfly patterns into a minimalist silhouette resting lightly atop the stone. Its lines stretch like wings catching the wind; its form glides light as a butterfly skimming water. There is no heavy, ornate carving — only the subtle charm of a fluttering butterfly shadow.

The artisan polishes meticulously along the stone's natural contour, translating the light, delicate brushwork of the original sketch into a warm, tangible piece of art. Worn close to the neck, it carries the crisp blue of a mountain stream and the grace of a woodland butterfly, exuding quiet elegance with a lasting, lingering charm.
