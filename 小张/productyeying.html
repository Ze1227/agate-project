叶萦

Yeying · Purple Agate Ring

紫玛瑙戒指

产品故事

主石：圆形弧面紫玛瑙，浓郁的深紫色调，是阜新玛瑙中的特色料种，也是这款产品的核心价值载体。

金属与造型：主石采用金色包边镶嵌，顶部设计了两片舒展的绿叶造型（可做珐琅工艺或彩宝点缀），呼应 "叶萦" 的命名 —— 取 "叶影萦回、石上生姿" 的东方自然意象。

结构细节：画面中的辅助线是珠宝设计的标准定位线，底部预留了小配石位，整体既可做吊坠挂坠，也可适配为戒指戒面。

Main Stone: A round cabochon purple agate in a rich deep purple hue. It is a signature variety of Fuxin agate and serves as the core value carrier of this piece.

Metalwork & Design: The central stone is secured in a gold-toned bezel setting, topped with two graceful leaf motifs that can be finished with enamel craftsmanship or accented with colored gemstones. The design echoes the name Yeying (Leaf Echo), drawing from the Eastern natural aesthetic of "lingering leaf shadows casting delicate charm upon the stone".

Structural Details: The guide lines in the sketch are standard positioning references for jewelry design. A small setting for an accent stone is reserved at the bottom. The overall design can be adapted into either a pendant charm or a ring center stone.

产地

辽宁阜新

解说

一方天然紫玛瑙，凝山川亿万年的沉淀，色如墨笔在宣纸上晕开的紫霞，温润沉敛，肌理天成。

设计师以东方水墨 "写意留白" 为法，删去传统雕饰的冗繁，只摘一片柔叶为形，轻覆于石顶。金勾边，翠点韵，线条疏朗如白描，形态婉转似晕染，没有直白的纹样堆砌，只留一缕叶影萦回的意趣。

匠人以刀代笔，循石之肌理细细雕琢，将手绘稿上的笔锋与温度，凝作方寸间的实物。戴于颈间，便似携了一片山间青叶、一汪石间紫玉，静气自生，余韵悠长。

This natural purple agate, forged over millions of years by the forces of mountains and waters, bears the hue of purple mist diffused by an ink brush across rice paper. It exudes a warm, understated elegance, with its texture entirely shaped by nature.

Drawing on the aesthetics of freehand brushwork and intentional negative space in Eastern ink wash painting, the designer strips away the ornateness of traditional carvings. Only a soft leaf silhouette rests gently atop the stone, framed by a gilded bezel with verdant enamel accents. Its clean lines echo the precision of line drawing, while its flowing contours evoke the soft gradation of ink wash. Rather than overt decorative patterns, it retains the subtle charm of lingering leaf shadows.

The artisan wields a chisel as a painter wields a brush, carving meticulously along the stone's natural grain. The brushstrokes and warmth of the original hand-drawn sketch are thus preserved in this tiny, tangible piece. Worn close to the skin, it carries the quiet grace of a mountain leaf and the depth of a stone-born purple gem, imparting serene poise with enduring elegance.
